## You may

1. **Run your own page.** Use Pelak to run a link page for yourself, your channel, your project or your own business, including a page that has a donation card, advertisements or links to things you sell.
2. **Personalise it.** For that page, change only these:
   - `site.config.js`, and `wrangler.jsonc` to set the worker name, bindings and domain
   - the images `public/logo.png` and `public/og.png`
   - the tips in `public/tips.js`
   - settings and secrets in your Cloudflare account
3. **Keep your copy on GitHub.** Keep a copy made with the Deploy button, the template button or a fork, public or private, so that your page can be deployed. Keep `LICENSE.md` and this file in it, unchanged.
